# Arch Prime /prime: section map (first draft)

## 1. What the page has to do

The reader is a Bitcoin holder who watched 2022 happen. They know LTV and liquidation, and they read looking for one overclaim that gives them a reason to leave. The one idea, landed on the first screen and proved below it: Arch Prime is one account where your Bitcoin can earn, back a dollar loan and trade, and you sign every step. The conversion is "Get early access": it costs an email, and one fact argues for it, that every loan here starts with someone's deposit. The current page asks ("Build the Bank") before it says what Prime is, runs four leads, lists the capabilities five times, and makes trust claims this reader checks: "still Bitcoin" (the app calls primeBTC a vault share) and "never wrapped" (misleading, since the aBTC underneath is bridged).

### How the page sits on arch.network

- **Who arrives.** From the nav, after home or /chain (home has no body link here): they need the account, not the thesis again. From X, cold: their first screen is the share card, which still says "Build the Bank…". From the footer's "Arch Prime" link, which today opens X.
- **Who it serves.** First the Bitcoin holder; second the dollar lender, whose aUSD funds the holder's loans. Builders exit to /chain and the docs; institutions and partners to the partner Typeform, via one link in §6.
- **Division of labour.** Home owns the thesis and the big $2T+. /chain owns how the network works: settlement, custody, liquidation. /prime owns the account: what a person does, what happens to their deposit, what can go wrong, and the ask. It links to /chain only on the claim that needs it, and never reuses /chain's card titles ("Borrow without selling.", "Put idle Bitcoin to work.").
- **Double duty.** Prime is the working evidence for Arch's thesis, carried in the structure: the reason (§2), the proof and its limit (§4), the direction (§7), the way back (§8).
- **Brand hierarchy.** Arch's marks sit only in the frame: nav, nav button, footer. Prime leads everything inside. Arch is named in words in §2, §4 and §7; a quiet "prime" footer variant signs off: "Arch Prime is built on Arch Network."
- **The exit.** "Get early access" opens a short sign-up (email, plus "Bitcoin or dollars"), never X (decision 3); when deposits open it becomes "Deposit". The desktop nav button and the mobile menu's hard-coded "Join the Ecosystem" both become "Get early access"; phones keep a slim pill, since the nav button is hidden below md. Not ready: /chain from §4 and §8, docs from §6.
- **Inbound fixes.** Point the footer's "Arch Prime" link here; restore /chain's commented-out "See it live" links, pointed here, once decision 2 settles; propose a link from home's "For users" cards; rewrite the share card to the hero, with an OG image from the towers.

## 2. The page in eight sentences

Arch Prime is one account where your Bitcoin can earn, back a dollar loan and trade, and you sign every step. Everywhere else Bitcoin has to pick a job and a venue, which is why so much sits idle; institutions have always had one account, and on Arch Network yours runs as a program, not a desk. That account puts every position on one screen, with the next job joining when it ships. Here is exactly what happens to your Bitcoin in each job, including who else can move it and where Arch's guarantees stop. Every one of those loans starts with someone's deposit, so the bank has to be built first, and early access, which costs an email, puts you in line. Before you sign up, the practical questions get plain answers. With today settled, the direction: the account starts with Bitcoin, and each market Arch brings to Bitcoin joins it. From an upper floor of the building you looked up at, the work goes on, and the ask is the same one step.

### 4. How it works: follow your Bitcoin

- **Job:** the mechanism and the page's one home for trust; proves Arch's thesis where it applies and says where it stops.
- **Question it answers:** Is this lending my Bitcoin to a platform? Who can move it, and how do I leave?
- **Message:** every step named, the risky ones too, with the layer that answers for each.
- **Content:**
  - Headline: (a) "Follow your Bitcoin in, and back out." (b) "What happens to your Bitcoin."
  - Opener: "Is this like lending your Bitcoin to a platform?" Answer: "A plain deposit is lent by a program, to borrowers who posted more collateral than they drew. primeBTC is different: a curator deploys most of it off chain, and you trust that curator."
  - Four tagged steps:
    - **01 Bring it onto Arch** (Arch Network). Bitcoin arrives as aBTC, dollars as aUSD. [Decision 2: what backs each, who can release it, how it returns.] You sign every move you make; two others can move funds: liquidators past 90%, and primeBTC's curator within its mandate. Inline /chain link.
    - **02 Put it to work.** Lend it (Arch Prime, on Arch Network's market): it lends to people borrowing Bitcoin against dollars and earns on the share that is lent, so yield depends on that demand. Or hold primeBTC (curator): a vault share, part held on chain as a reserve, the rest off chain; its value moves with each curator report and can fall. This path says in words that it sits outside Arch Network's guarantees.
    - **03 Borrow against it** (Arch Prime sets the terms; Arch Network clears liquidation). Post aBTC, draw aUSD, up to 80% loan to value. "Past 90% the position liquidates, for everyone, with no exceptions." [Decision 1: whether collateral keeps earning.] primeBTC can't back a loan yet (§3's Next). Inline /chain link.
    - **04 Take it back** (Arch Prime). A lent deposit withdraws when the market has liquidity. primeBTC redeems to aBTC: the reserve pays at once, larger requests join a queue you can see and cancel, and an emergency pause can hold everything.
  - "What you rely on": up to 80% loan to value, liquidation past 90%; the price feed that sets your loan-to-value; what backs aBTC and aUSD, and who can release them; the payout order (reserve, then queue); the curator (primeBTC only); the contracts and their audit status; Bitcoin's price; fees before you sign, and ARCH for network fees.
  - Last line, an open question: "Someone has to fund these loans."
- **From the current page:** replaces the pinned "How primeBTC works." Keep "Withdraw any time." as an idea, with the queue, and "Redeem to aBTC". Cut "Still Bitcoin. More yield.", "Never locked, never wrapped.", "Use its value. Keep the yield.", "nothing leaves Bitcoin", "from the first block".
- **Bridge in, out:** in on "against what, on what terms?"; out on "Someone has to fund these loans."
- **Layout:** opener full width; then on desktop the headline stays fixed left while steps scroll right (sticky, not pinned), tags as small labels at each step's head, the spec list closing in two columns. Phone: one column.
- **Pacing:** the longest read, in plain scroll; the reader is checking, not watching. **CTA:** none.
- **Watch-outs:** step 01 waits for decision 2: a sentence Arch can say next to /chain's "Never an IOU. Never a bridge claim." (the app calls aBTC and aUSD bridged).

## 9. Layout principles for this page

1. **The first frame answers the 2022 reader:** promise, custody clause, button and "not open to everyone yet" at 1440×900 and 390×844, before the photo moves.
2. **One persistent object: §2's diagram**, returning once in §7. §3 to §5 carry the story in words and screens, with no morphing device between the bookends.
3. **Two full-bleed photographs, one building:** street, then upper floor. The footer drops its image so the view is last.
4. **Survey once, go deep once.** Capabilities appear together only in §3; §4 follows the balance, not the verbs.
5. **Every number sits next to its mechanism.** 80% and 90% only in §4; $2T+ as half a sentence in §2.
6. **Tags show where Arch's guarantees apply, and risk is body copy.** /chain links sit only on Arch Network steps, never on primeBTC; §4's risk lines carry a step's weight and never collapse on phone.
7. **The unshipped looks unshipped.** §3's Next is one line, no screen, no link; §7 is type and two real marks.
8. **One label, three placements, one next-step line.** Desktop nav button and phone-only pill carry the ask between them.
9. **Phone keeps the order of belief:** copy before towers in the hero, one screen then text in §3, §4's opener before its steps.
